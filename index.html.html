Telecommunications Customer Churn Dashboard

End-to-End Cloud Data Engineering, SQL Transformation & Interactive Business Intelligence

Google BigQuery SQL Data Cleaning Looker Studio HTML5 & CSS3

Project Data Pipeline

  • Data Warehouse: Ingested 7,043 customer records into Google BigQuery (customer_data.Telco).
  • SQL Cleaning: Handled empty spaces in TotalCharges via SAFE_CAST to FLOAT64 and standardized boolean Churn flags into Yes/No categories.
  • Feature Engineering: Created lifecycle Tenure Group buckets and custom churn rate aggregations in Looker Studio.

Key Business Insights

  • Churn Definition: Customers who cancelled their telecommunications subscription within the last month (~26.5% baseline).
  • Contract Risk: Month-to-month subscribers drive the vast majority of customer attrition compared to 1- and 2-year contracts.
  • Early Lifecycle Vulnerability: Customers in their first 0–6 months show the steepest churn rate, highlighting the need for early onboarding retention.
